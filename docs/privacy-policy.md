Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the QUANTI Discord bot.

1. Information QUANTI May Receive

Depending on the features and Discord permissions in use, QUANTI may receive information including:

- Discord user IDs.
- Usernames and display names.
- Server and channel IDs.
- Commands and interactions sent to the bot.
- Message content intentionally provided to QUANTI.
- Timestamps and basic technical information necessary to operate the bot.

QUANTI does not automatically have access to information that Discord does not provide through the permissions granted to the bot.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

- Respond to commands and messages.
- Generate AI responses.
- Operate bot features.
- Maintain security and prevent abuse.
- Troubleshoot errors.
- Improve reliability and functionality.

3. AI Processing

Content submitted to QUANTI may be transmitted to an artificial intelligence provider when necessary to generate a response.

Only information needed to fulfill the requested interaction should be sent for processing.

Third-party AI providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Data Storage

QUANTI may store limited information when necessary for configuration, functionality, security, moderation, troubleshooting, or other enabled features.

Not every message or interaction is necessarily stored permanently.

5. Sensitive Information

Do not send QUANTI passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, financial information, private keys, or other highly sensitive personal information.

6. Sharing and Sale of Information

QUANTI does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the bot, process requests, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or provide requested functionality.

7. Discord

QUANTI operates through Discord and is also subject to information and permissions provided by Discord.

Discord independently controls information collected through its own platform.

8. Security

Reasonable efforts may be used to protect information associated with QUANTI, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Requests

Users may request information about stored data or request deletion of data associated with them where technically possible and legally appropriate.

Requests may be submitted through QUANTI's official support or contact channel.

10. Children's Privacy

QUANTI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Discord's age requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as QUANTI develops or gains new features. The effective date above will reflect the current version.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be submitted through QUANTI's official support or contact channel.
